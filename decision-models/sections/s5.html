Every decision model is a model you would have served anyway (run behind an API so other software can call it) plus some amount of change, and the change comes in two forms: post-training, continuing the model's weights on new data, or a head, a new layer trained on top of the model's hidden state. The amount of change is the whole taxonomy: how far are you from the LLM you would have served anyway? Zero (you only read a field), small (you retrained the same model), structural (you bolted a new head on), or total (you swapped the backbone). Each step further buys something and costs something, and the next section is just picking a rung.
